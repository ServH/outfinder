2. Screenshot Science

Rules backed by ASO research (SensorTower, AppTweak, StoreMaven data).

CRITICAL First 3 Screenshots

On iOS 17+, only the first 3 screenshots are visible in search results without tapping. They must tell a complete story:

  • SS1: Hero feature (Outfit Visualizer) — instant differentiation
  • SS2: Content depth (159 colors) — "there's a lot here"
  • SS3: Core mechanic (combinations) — "I understand how it works"

If the user scrolls past 3, they're already interested. SS4-6 close the deal.

TIP Caption Hierarchy

Every screenshot has two text elements:

  • Headline (large): Benefit-oriented, emotional. Answers "what's in it for me?" — not "what does this screen do"
  • Subtitle (small): Feature-oriented, specific. Adds credibility

Wrong: "Outfit Visualizer Screen"
Right: "See your outfit before you dress"

DATA Visual Consistency

All screenshots share:

  • Same warm gradient overlay for captions (matching app's Wada aesthetic)
  • Noto Serif JP for headlines (brand continuity)
  • Dark text-on-light-overlay top gradient
  • No device frames — Apple removed them from listings; raw content converts better now
  • Real app content, not mockups — Apple rejects fake screenshots

TIP Required Sizes

iPhone screenshots required for App Store submission:

  • 6.7" (iPhone 15 Pro Max): 1290 × 2796px — required
  • 6.5" (iPhone 14 Plus): 1284 × 2778px — optional but recommended
  • 5.5" (iPhone 8 Plus): 1242 × 2208px — required if supporting older devices

Minimum 3 screenshots, maximum 10. We use 6 (sweet spot: tells full story without fatigue).

CRITICAL What Apple Rejects

  • Screenshots that don't represent real app functionality
  • Device bezels that don't match current hardware
  • Text-only slides (must show actual app UI)
  • Pricing in screenshots (prices vary by region)
  • Mention of competing apps
  • Use of "free" if there's any IAP

DATA Localization Strategy

For launch, we prepare two sets:

  • English (US): Primary market. Captions in English
  • Spanish (ES): Alejandro's market. Translated captions

Same screenshots, different caption overlays. The app's Japanese elements stay — they're the brand identity, not a localization issue.

4. Keywords (100 chars max)

CRITICAL Comma-separated, no spaces after commas. Don't repeat words from the app name or subtitle — Apple already indexes those.

English Keywords

palette,wardrobe,styling,fashion,harmony,coordinate,combination,dress,match,clothing,capsule
93 characters. Avoids: "color" (in title), "outfit" (in subtitle), "wada" (in subtitle)

Spanish Keywords

paleta,armario,estilismo,moda,combinar,vestir,ropa,coordinacion,capsula,tendencia,prendas

Keyword Strategy

  • "capsule" — targets capsule wardrobe audience (high intent, low competition)
  • "harmony" — differentiates from generic color picker apps
  • "coordinate" — action verb, matches search intent "coordinate outfit colors"
  • "styling" — broad fashion term with decent volume
  • "match" — highest volume term: "what colors match"

Competitor Gap

Main competitors and where Outfinder wins:

  • Coolors — design tool, not fashion. We own "outfit" + "wardrobe"
  • Pantone Connect — professional/expensive. We own "free" + "daily"
  • Dresso / Cladwell — photo-based wardrobe. We own "color theory" + "Wada"
  • No competitor combines curated historical palettes + outfit visualization

6. Screenshot Production Plan

How to produce the actual screenshots for submission.

Option A: Simulator Captures + Figma Overlay

  • Run app in iPhone 15 Pro Max simulator
  • Take raw screenshots (Cmd+S in Simulator)
  • Add caption overlays in Figma (or Sketch/Canva)
  • Export at 1290 x 2796px

Recommended. Guarantees pixel-accurate app UI. Captions are the only overlay.

Option B: Programmatic with fastlane snapshot

  • Configure fastlane snapshot with UI tests
  • Auto-captures in all required sizes
  • frameit adds captions and device frames
  • Fully automated, reproducible

Better for updates but higher initial setup cost. Consider for v1.1+.

Caption Overlay Template

Each screenshot gets a gradient overlay at top or bottom:

  • Gradient: dark → transparent (same direction as content allows)
  • Headline: Noto Serif JP, 38px @3x, white (#fafaf8)
  • Subtitle: Inter, 22px @3x, white at 65% opacity
  • Padding: 64px top, 40px sides (@3x)
