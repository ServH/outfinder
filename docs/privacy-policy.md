# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-03-24

This privacy policy applies to the Outfinder app ("Application") for iOS, created by Alejandro Camps ("Service Provider").

## No Data Collection

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The Application is designed to work entirely offline.

All user data — including saved favorites and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Service Provider or any third party.

## In-App Purchases

The Application offers an optional one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The Service Provider does not receive, process, or store any payment information. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase transaction.

Purchase status is verified through RevenueCat, a third-party service that communicates with Apple's servers to confirm your entitlement. RevenueCat does not receive any personally identifiable information from the Application. For more information, see [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## No Analytics or Tracking

The Application does not use analytics services, advertising frameworks, crash reporting tools, or any form of user tracking.

## No Third-Party Data Sharing

The Application does not share any data with third parties, except as described in the In-App Purchases section above.

## Local Data

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

- **Favorites:** The combination IDs you have saved (stored in AsyncStorage)
- **Premium status:** Whether you have purchased the premium upgrade (stored in the device Keychain via Secure Store)
- **Onboarding status:** Whether you have completed the onboarding flow

You can delete all local data by uninstalling the Application from your device.

## Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children. The Application is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
